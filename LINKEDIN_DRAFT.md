I built Morocco AE, a local invoice and quarterly tax-planning tool for Moroccan auto-entrepreneurs.

It combines a bilingual invoice generator with a reserve planner that separates cash already received from estimates for future months. It also shows the statutory turnover limits and flags the same-client services threshold for review.

I built it with React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS. It runs in the browser without an account or backend. The README explains the official sources and the tax rules that still need case-specific confirmation.
